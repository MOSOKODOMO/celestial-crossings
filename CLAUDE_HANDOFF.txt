WHAT EXISTS NOW

1. Fortune reader at / or /index.html

- Thai is the default; the reader and content support Thai and English.
- Birthday entry supports Gregorian CE and Thai Buddhist Era BE years, with
  invalid-date and future-birthday checks.
- Birth time can be exact, approximate or unknown; unknown is a separate state.
- Free weekly readings cover this week or next week. Specific-day readings accept
  today or a future day. Custom-week access is an unavailable future feature.
- Optional ordinary-day, interview and date contexts; balanced, preparation,
  confidence and communication guidance.
- Deterministic readings: identical relevant inputs produce the same story IDs.
  Changing language or context guidance does not reroll the fortune.
- Library of 52 core fortunes, 64 topic passages and 14 supporting notes, plus
  contextual guidance and bilingual imagined future chapters.
- Future chapters are age-aware stories covering career, family/connection,
  wealth and legacy. They are stable across reading modes and use birthday/age.
- Browse/search the story library, explicitly load sample details, download a
  reading as text, print, and view the printable bilingual catalogue.
- Responsive mobile landing experience and a simpler desktop reading form.
- Birth details remain in memory and reset on reload. No account, analytics,
  remote fonts or payment integration.

The fortune engine uses invented editorial rules: 52 birthday bands, six
four-hour windows, a weekly rotation and stable hashed passage selection. It is
not a traditional Thai birth chart or a validated prediction system. Preserve
that distinction. Astrology integration and practitioner review are future work.

2. Card collection preview at /collect.html

- Fully illustrated 30-card deck: six categories times five rarity ranks.
- Categories and animal identities:
  Love — cream moon bunny; Career — tiger cub; Wealth — golden kitten;
  Wellbeing — baby capybara; Connection — baby elephant; Growth — baby deer.
- Choose exactly two intentions, then choose one of three face-down cards.
- Mouse, touch and keyboard interaction; hover/focus lift; flip and reveal;
  reduced-motion handling; card title, category, rarity and reflective meaning.
- All three positions have identical odds. Each choice draws one card.
- Result is committed to browser storage before the animation so reloading or

DRAW RULES TO PRESERVE

Rarity is rolled independently every time:
SSR 0.15%; SR 2.85%; S 12%; R 30%; C 55%.

Ordinary category draws are equally weighted across all six categories.
Preferences stay fixed within each group of ten draws. If none of the first nine
cards matches either chosen category, the tenth is selected equally from the two
preferred categories. An earlier match fulfils that group's guarantee, so the
tenth can then be any category. Draw eleven starts a new group; preferences can
change between groups. This guarantees a category match, not an SSR or one card
from each preferred category. Rarity odds never change for the guarantee.

The browser uses crypto.getRandomValues. It remains a client-side simulation,
with no purchase, server verification, account or durable inventory service.
Keep the localStorage key "celestial-atelier-preview-v1" unless deliberately
migrating saved collections; it was retained through the MooJai rename.
